## 2. Peak / Masterpiece 10
*Unrivaled artistic zenith, flawless thematic resonance, and immortal sequential art.*

- **Berserk** — Kentarou Miura
  - Progress: 364 / 364 chapters · 41 volumes · ~25.5h
  - Rating: 10 / 10 (Masterpiece)
  - Notes: The undisputed apex of dark fantasy. Kentarou Miura's pen-and-ink detailing is biblical in scope. Guts' enduring will against causality, the tragedy of the Eclipse, and the Golden Age make this the gold standard of manga.

- **Vagabond** — Takehiko Inoue
  - Progress: 327 / 327 chapters · 37 volumes · ~21.3h
  - Rating: 10 / 10 (Masterpiece)
  - Notes: Takehiko Inoue's brushwork breathes profound Zen philosophy into Miyamoto Musashi's journey. The transition from bloodlust to the agrarian soil of the Farming Arc stands among the most transcendental arcs in literature.

- **Vinland Saga** — Makoto Yukimura
  - Progress: 216 / 216 chapters · 28 volumes · ~13.0h
  - Rating: 10 / 10 (Masterpiece)
  - Notes: A Viking revenge tale that transforms into a monumental meditation on pacifism, forgiveness, and the cost of human violence. The Farmland Arc (Ketil's farm) redefines Thorfinn forever. "I have no enemies."

## Taste Notes

### Yes
- Deep psychological character drama, philosophical growth, and high-consequence narratives (Berserk, Vagabond, Vinland Saga).
- Sensational panel compositions, dynamic ink brushwork, and auteur art styles (Takehiko Inoue, Kentarou Miura, Tatsuki Fujimoto).
- Well-earned romantic progression with emotional maturity and natural chemistry (Blue Box).
- Clean, tactile power progression and top-tier vertical webtoon fight staging (Solo Leveling).

### No
- Senseless harem tropes with zero character agency or perpetual misunderstandings.
- Generic isekai with unearned overpowered cheat skills and flat villain caricatures.
- Padding and filler arcs that dilute high-stakes narrative momentum.
